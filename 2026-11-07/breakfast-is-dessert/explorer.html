At 25%, most adult breakfasts (55.5%) and two-thirds of kids' breakfasts (67.7%) are past the line. At the median snack's own sweetness (34.6%), 38% of adult breakfasts are still past it. The median adult breakfast is 27.2% sugar calories; the median dinner is 10.9%.

Data: NHANES dietary recalls, cycles 2007-08 to 2017-18. Adults 20–79 (n = 24,156 person-days); children 2–19. Total sugars, not added sugars — see the README for the fruit/milk caveat and the food-group decomposition.
