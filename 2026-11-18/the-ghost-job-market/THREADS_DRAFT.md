I counted the whole tech job market this morning. 8,371 open roles across 40 companies.

A third of it isn't real.

31% of postings have been open 90+ days. At Databricks and Scale AI, it's over half. One Databricks sales role has been "open" for 1,071 days.

The live market is 5,776 jobs, not 8,371. Discount every board by a third before you believe it.

Three more things the census turned up:

Sales is hiring 2.7x faster than data science. Design is 1.6% of all postings. The build phase is over, the sell phase is on.

Junior roles are 1.5% of the market. Senior and above are 36%. The ladder's bottom rung is basically gone.

And remote didn't die, it consolidated. Eight companies are 82-100% remote. Eighteen are under 5%. If you want remote, pick the company, not the filter.
