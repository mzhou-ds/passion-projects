When will you peak?

The cross-section says strength peaks at 24. Careers say otherwise: in 2,774 long powerlifting careers, peak age moved almost one-for-one with the age a lifter started competing. Enter a start age and see the career clock, not the textbook curve.


Estimates from OpenIPF raw full-power careers with 5+ meets over 8+ years (regression slope 0.89 overall; women 0.93, men 0.87). A later peak is a lower peak on average: median career-best Goodlift falls from ~93 for teenage starters to ~71 for 50-something starters. Lifters still competing at 60 held a median 95.8% of their own peak.
