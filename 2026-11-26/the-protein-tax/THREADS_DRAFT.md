I went looking for the protein tax and found the opposite.

Everyone says the protein craze is crowding out fiber. I measured it across 4,576 products on US shelves. Protein-branded foods carry 80% more fiber per calorie than the plain versions.

The protein bar is not the candy bar. The plain bar is. Median plain bar: 25 grams of sugar, 73% score Nutri-Score D or E. Median protein bar: 10 grams of sugar, 8 grams of fiber.

The exchange rate is positive. Each extra gram of protein comes with 0.24 grams more fiber. Protein pasta gets its protein from chickpeas and lentils. That is why it brings fiber with it.

A day of swaps, cereal plus yogurt plus a bar, gains 1.3 grams of fiber. And 25 grams of protein you almost certainly do not need. 93% of Americans already hit their protein target. 9.5% hit their fiber target.

Buy the protein bar for the fiber and the sugar, not the protein. And happy Thanksgiving. One plate tonight is 67 grams of protein and 12 grams of fiber. The green beans are doing more work than they look.
