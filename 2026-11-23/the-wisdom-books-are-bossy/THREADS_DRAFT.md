The Tao Te Ching has nine commands in the whole book.

Six of them start with "let."

I counted the orders in 13 wisdom texts, East and West, sentence by sentence. The East barely commands at all. The Tao is 1.8% commands. The Analects, the actual rulebook, is 1.4%, the lowest in the corpus.

The bossiest book is a diary nobody was supposed to read. Marcus Aurelius orders himself around in 11.5% of the Meditations, and half of those orders are addressed to "thou." Stoicism is literally self-command.

The Bible's wisdom books command by forbidding. 54% of Proverbs' commands are "do not." The Tao commands by permitting. Emerson is 46% "let."

"Let" is the most common command verb in the canon. Not do. Not seek. Let.
