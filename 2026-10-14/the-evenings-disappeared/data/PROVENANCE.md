# Provenance

- `gss_extract.csv` - 48-variable extract of the General Social Survey
  cumulative cross-section file 1972-2024 (NORC at the University of
  Chicago), downloaded as `gss_all.rda` from Kieran Healy's `gssr`
  mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kjhealy/gssr/master/data/gss_all.rda
  (23,065,060 bytes at fetch time, 2026-10-14) and extracted with
  `src/extract_gss.R`. Full file: 75,699 respondents x 6,942 variables.
- `atus_time_by_age_owid.csv` - Our World in Data grapher export of
  "time spent with relationships by age", built from U.S. Bureau of
  Labor Statistics American Time Use Survey diaries:
  https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/time-spent-with-relationships-by-age-us.csv
- `atus_respondent_extract.csv` - derived by `src/05_atus.py` from the
  BLS ATUS respondent microdata files for 2003, 2019, and 2024:
  https://www.bls.gov/tus/datafiles/atusresp_2003.zip (underscore in
  the 2003 filename), .../atusresp-2019.zip, .../atusresp-2024.zip.
  Raw .dat files (about 20 MB) are not committed; place them in
  `data/raw/` to rebuild the extract. Columns kept: TUYEAR, TUDIARYDAY,
  TRSPPRES, TUFINLWGT, TRTALONE, TRTFRIEND, TRTSPOUSE, TRTUNMPART,
  TRTCHILD, TRTFAMILY (37,824 respondents).
- Note: bls.gov direct downloads were blocked from this machine's
  shell network (Akamai 403) during the build; the ATUS files were
  fetched through the browser instead. The GSS/OWID routes above are
  canonical mirrors and are what `src/01_fetch.py` uses.
- All other CSV/JSON files in this folder are generated by
  `src/02_analysis.py`, `src/03_model.py`, and `src/05_atus.py`.
