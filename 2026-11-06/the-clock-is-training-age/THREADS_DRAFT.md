## Post 1
Your strength peak isn't at 30. It's about a year and a half after you start, at whatever age you start. I ran the numbers on 331,000 powerlifters. Start at 30 and you lose nothing vs starting at 20. The bill doesn't show up until about 45.

## Post 2 (follow-up in the same thread)
The wild part: the median lifter hits 95% of their lifetime best within a year of their first meet. And 48% compete exactly once and never come back. The sport's problem isn't biology. It's retention.
