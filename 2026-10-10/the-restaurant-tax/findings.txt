Build #34 — The Restaurant Tax (nutrition) — 2026-10-10
NHANES 2015-2018 pooled, adults 20-65, reliable Day-1 recall, analytic n=7,413 (112,370 food records).

HEADLINE NUMBERS
- Where calories come from (survey-weighted, WTDRD1/2): store 64.7%, fast food 15.7%, full-service restaurant 9.4%, other 10.1%. 53.2% ate some restaurant calories that day; among them, restaurants were 45.4% of the day's energy. Dinner/supper is 35.7% of all calories; full-service is 53.1% dinner, fast food splits lunch 38.7% / dinner 40.7%. Grain products 35.2% of national calories; grains + meat mixtures are 66.8% of restaurant calories.
- The restaurant tax (per 1,000 kcal, person-bootstrap 95% CI): sodium — store 1,531 mg (1,508-1,553), fast food 1,899 (+24.0%), full-service 2,150 (+40.4%, saltiest source on the board). Fiber — store 8.4 g, fast food 6.5 (-22.7%), full-service 7.0 (-16.8%). Saturated fat — fast food +21.2% vs store, full-service +11.1%.
- Sugar is the grocery store's tax: total sugar per 1,000 kcal — store 55.7 g (highest), fast food 35.6 (-36.1%), full-service 28.4 (-48.9%). Added sugars are not in the pooled NHANES files, so total sugar is used.
- Adjusted models (n=6,387; controls: age, sex, race/ethnicity, education, income-to-poverty, cycle, total kcal): per +10pp restaurant calorie share — BMI +0.19 (p=5.7e-9), obesity OR 1.048 (1.029-1.067, p=3.6e-7), dysglycemia OR 1.047 (p=7.8e-6), HbA1c +0.014 (p=0.003). Threshold shape: 1-15% share OR 0.86 (p=0.197, n.s.), 15-30% OR 1.01 (p=0.884, n.s.), 30%+ OR 1.28 (p<0.001).
- XGBoost obesity AUC 0.673 vs logistic 0.667 — boosting earns ~nothing; both reported. SHAP: NH Asian 0.329, age 0.168, education 0.140; top dietary signal sodium density 0.135, ahead of total calories 0.096 and income 0.074.
- Archetypes (K-means k=5 by silhouette): Home-Base Cooks 17.2% (86% store, fiber 15.3 g/1,000 kcal, obesity 32.9% — but oldest at 46.3y and highest HbA1c 5.86; reported, not explained away). Drive-Thru Regulars 20.9% (obesity 47.8%). Sit-Down Splurgers 13.5% (richest, 3.09x poverty, obesity 45.1%). Pantry Salt Loaders 27.9%-largest (84% store, sodium 1,932 mg/1,000 kcal, sat fat 14.8 g). Grocery Sweet Tooths 20.6% (sugar 80.7 g/1,000 kcal, poorest, 2.18x poverty).
- Counterfactual (fast-food calories repriced at store nutrient profile, calories fixed, population-weighted): sodium -127.6 mg/person/day (-3.5% of mean 3,666 mg), fiber +0.7 g (+3.8%), saturated fat -0.9 g (-3.2%), but sugar +7.0 g (+6.4%) — the swap trades salt for sugar. Arithmetic identity, not a causal forecast.

CAVEATS: cross-sectional; one Day-1 recall (eat-out days heavier by construction); self-report under-reporting; restaurant code 5 ("no additional information") grouped into "other"; source is where food was obtained, not who cooked it (meal kits blur store/restaurant); population shares weighted WTDRD1/2, person-level models unweighted with HC1 SEs; silence at low restaurant shares is absence of evidence, not proof of safety.
