Build #44 - The Error Bar Is Nine Years Wide (biohacking) - 2026-10-20
PhenoAge (Levine 2018) rebuilt for 4,788 NHANES 2015-2018 adults with all 9 labs; noise from EFLM within-person biological variation (CVi) + assay CVa, Monte Carlo per person.
1) Median within-person SD 2.34 y; median 95% interval 9.1 y wide (IQR 8.6-9.7); 99.5% of intervals >4 y, 97.0% >8 y; for 48.8% of people the interval covers exactly-average aging, so one panel cannot separate them from normal.
2) Noise decomposition (one-at-a-time and leave-one-out agree): RDW 68.9% of noise variance, glucose 10.2%, creatinine 4.6%, WBC 4.0%, lymphocyte % 3.7%, MCV 3.6%, CRP only 3.6% despite a 42.5% total CV (log transform + 0.0954 coefficient mute it); RDW wins via the model's largest coefficient (0.3306 per point) on a quiet marker (CV 3.8%).
3) Pure retest, zero true change: 55.8% of people move >=2 y either way; 27.2% "rejuvenate" >=2 y; 28.6% "age" >=2 y; two draws rank people at r=0.94 and 81.6% of top-quartile "accelerated" labels survive.
4) Regression to the mean, buyers' band (|accel| <= 10 y, n=1,604): top-decile first readings fall 2.1 y on retest, top quartile 1.2 y, bottom quartile rises 1.4 y, nothing intervened. Rejuvant (Demidenko 2021, n=42, no control, claimed -7.96 y) reports its benefit concentrated in the oldest at baseline, the RTM fingerprint.
5) Claims vs band: CALERIE RCT (n=220, 2 y) found zero effect on DNAm PhenoAge/GrimAge and 2-3% on DunedinPACE (a rate); Fitzgerald 2021 (n=43) -1.96 y within-group / -3.23 y vs control (Horvath). Group means average noise down; a personal two-panel delta does not.
6) Between-person xb variance: age term 62.6%; of the rest, state markers (red-cell/iron 9.1, glucose 8.1, inflammation 3.7 points) beat the slow background (16.5 points); RDW ties glucose as largest single non-age term, corr(RDW, accel) = 0.51, corr(albumin, accel) = -0.57.
7) Averaging buys precision: 6 panels -> 4 y interval, 21 panels -> 2 y, 83 -> 1 y. Sensitivity: halving all CVi still leaves a 4.5 y median interval; doubling CRP CV moves 9.1 -> 9.4 y; halving RDW CV cuts it to 6.3 y.
