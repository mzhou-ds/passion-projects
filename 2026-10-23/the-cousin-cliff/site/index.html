The Cousin Cliff

Cousins = aunts & uncles × their kids. Move either leg and watch the product move twice as fast. Musing with Mike.

Expected first cousins
Reunion kin (2 parents + 4 grandparents + siblings + aunts/uncles + cousins)

Presets come from the period-fertility kinship model in this project (UN World Population Prospects TFR via Our World in Data; generation gap 29 years). "Child of two only-child parents" is an arithmetic scenario, not a measurement: if both parents have no siblings, first cousins through them are zero whatever the local fertility rate is. The reunion count is the size of the tree at birth, before mortality - not the size of any real room in any real year.
