Biohacking talks about upside. Lifespan, energy, focus. Nobody talks about the other side of the ledger.

I put the whole stack into the FDA's adverse-event database and counted what came back. 16 molecules. 9,386 reports. 62% serious. 562 deaths named.

One molecule stands apart. Methylene blue, the focus drop everyone is taking. Its number one adverse event is serotonin syndrome. 455 reports. 29% of everything filed against it. 2.5% of every serotonin-syndrome report in the entire FDA database names methylene blue.

The FDA warned about this in 2011. Methylene blue blocks the enzyme that breaks down serotonin. Mix it with an SSRI and serotonin can pile up to fatal levels. The cases were IV doses in surgery. The FDA said the oral risk was not known.

It is still not known. And now the molecule is sold in dropper bottles to people who overlap heavily with SSRI users.

The rest of the shelf is quieter but not quiet. Berberine: 1,014 reports, 61 deaths named. DHEA: 2,308 reports. Quercetin: 1,549. And the reports are compounding. Berberine's grew 20x since 2012 while the whole database grew 2.5x.

The filter I would actually use: reports per published trial. Creatine has 2,872 trials and 0.4 reports per trial. Pregnenolone has 38 trials and 10.3 per trial. BPC-157 and lithium orotate have zero trials and reports anyway.
