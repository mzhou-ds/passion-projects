# Build #71 — The Side-Effect Shelf
# FDA FAERS (openFDA drug event), ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed. Data fetched 2026-11-17.
# 24 compounds: 16 on the biohacking shelf, 5 controls, 3 pharma benchmarks.
# FAERS counts are reports naming the molecule as the SUSPECT drug, 2004-Oct 2026.
# "Deaths named" = death was the reported outcome, not adjudicated causation.
# No denominators: these are counts, not incidence rates.

1. METHYLENE BLUE'S #1 ADVERSE EVENT IS SEROTONIN SYNDROME. 455 reports list
   serotonin syndrome, 29.1% of its 1,564 suspect reports, and 2.5% of all
   18,029 serotonin-syndrome suspect reports in FAERS name methylene blue.
   Its serious share is 92.8%, the highest of all 24 compounds in the build
   (sirolimus 85.8%, caffeine 84.2%, BPC-157 87.5%). 247 deaths named, 935
   hospitalizations, 338 drug-interaction reports. 93% of its reports are
   filed by clinicians (56% other health professionals, 29% physicians),
   reflecting hospital IV use, not supplement use. FDA warned about this
   exact interaction in a Drug Safety Communication, July 2011 (updated Oct
   2011): methylene blue inhibits MAO-A; cases were mostly IV 1-8 mg/kg
   during surgery in patients on SSRIs, SNRIs, or clomipramine; oral risk
   stated as not known.

2. THE SHELF, COUNTED. 9,386 suspect reports across 16 molecules; 5,789
   serious (61.7%); 2,539 hospitalizations; 685 life-threatening; 476
   disability; 562 deaths named. By compound: DHEA 2,308 (53% serious, 70
   deaths), methylene blue 1,564 (93%, 247), quercetin 1,549 (51%, 45),
   resveratrol 1,201 (53%, 57), berberine 1,014 (59%, 61), taurine 852
   (70%, 61; seizure 46), pregnenolone 392 (54%, 6), ashwagandha 334
   (51%, 13), lithium orotate 80 (59%, 0). Methylene blue alone is 44% of
   the shelf's deaths and 37% of its hospitalizations. Everywhere else the
   top reactions are generic: fatigue, nausea, headache, diarrhoea.

3. THE REPORTS ARE COMPOUNDING. 2012 to 2025: berberine 10 to 206 reports
   per year (20.6x, ~26% CAGR), quercetin 9 to 283 (31.4x, ~30% CAGR),
   creatine 10 to 139 (13.9x), melatonin 493 to 6,264 (12.7x), methylene
   blue 39 to 241 (6.2x), caffeine 499 to 2,443 (4.9x). The whole FAERS
   database grew only 2.5x over the same period (551,378 to 1,386,644).
   Berberine's reporting grew ~8x faster than the database; quercetin's
   ~12x faster.

4. EVIDENCE DENSITY IS THE DISCRIMINATOR. Reports per published RCT:
   creatine 0.40 (2,872 RCTs, 1,139 reports), NMN 0.24 (17 RCTs, 4
   reports), urolithin A 0.22 (27 RCTs, 6 reports), NR 1.35 (31 RCTs, 42
   reports), methylene blue 3.93 (398 RCTs, 1,564 reports), DHEA 3.58
   (644 RCTs, 2,308 reports), taurine 3.52 (242 RCTs, 852 reports),
   resveratrol 4.38 (274 RCTs, 1,201 reports), ashwagandha 6.19 (54 RCTs,
   334 reports), quercetin 6.40 (242 RCTs, 1,549 reports), berberine 8.97
   (113 RCTs, 1,014 reports), pregnenolone 10.32 (38 RCTs, 392 reports).
   BPC-157: 24 reports, 0 RCTs, 3 registered trials. Lithium orotate: 80
   reports, 0 RCTs, 2 registered trials.

5. TWO WORLDS OF HARM VISIBILITY. Consumer-filed share of reports:
   lithium orotate 81%, quercetin 68%, ashwagandha 64%, resveratrol 61%,
   berberine 58%, pregnenolone 56%, DHEA 53%, creatine 48%, taurine 48%.
   Report-weighted across the shelf, about half of all reports are filed
   by consumers (4,261 of 8,576 with reporter data). Methylene blue: 7%
   consumer. Sirolimus: 10% consumer. The shelf's safety signal lives
   outside the medical literature; methylene blue's lives inside hospitals.

6. WHAT DID NOT SHOW UP. Liver-injury reactions in direct-search counts:
   creatine 32, taurine 22, DHEA 16, berberine 15, resveratrol 11,
   quercetin 10, ashwagandha 5. Small everywhere; the published
   ashwagandha liver-injury cases do not dominate FAERS. Serotonin
   syndrome for every other shelf compound: 0 to 1 reports, except taurine
   (7) and creatine (5). Methylene blue's 455 is a total outlier, 65x the
   next-highest shelf compound.

7. DATA ARTIFACTS FLAGGED, NOT HIDDEN. DHEA reports collapse from
   75-392/year (2012-2021) to 8-12/year (2022-2026) with no query change;
   treated as a reporting/product-name break, DHEA excluded from trend
   conclusions. Vitamin D (341,660), magnesium (221,916), melatonin
   (57,174), caffeine (24,796), metformin (437,440) totals are inflated by
   co-reporting in hospitalized patients on many drugs; they are not used
   to normalize the shelf. 2026 is a partial year (openFDA data through
   Oct 28, 2026). Methylene blue drug-interaction count differs slightly
   by endpoint: 338 (count) vs 347 (direct search); 338 is cited.
