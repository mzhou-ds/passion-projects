Your doctor takes three numbers at every checkup. Blood pressure, weight, pulse.

None of them predicts whether you die.

I ran the contest on 6,085 Americans from NHANES, followed for up to 8 years. Grip strength beat all three combined. One standard deviation of grip cuts mortality risk 55%. BMI and systolic blood pressure carried literally zero signal once age was in the model.

The price: a 10-kilo weaker handshake carries the mortality risk of being 6 years older.

Men slide down a gradient. The weakest fifth die at 5.8x the rate of the strongest. Women fall off a cliff. Only the weakest fifth is at risk.

And it is not just a proxy for working out. Adjusting for strength training barely moves the number. The signal lives in the muscle.
