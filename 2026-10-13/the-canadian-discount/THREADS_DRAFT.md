In 2011, Canada was richer than America per person. $52,286 vs $50,008. Oil boom, loonie at par.

In 2024: $55,209 vs $86,417. Canada makes 64 cents on the US dollar now. At PPP, 75 cents. The IMF forecast has it stuck there through 2030.

The part that got me: 2019 to 2024, Canada's total economy grew 9.4%. Population grew 9.5%. Per person, five years of running in place. The US grew 9.5% per person in the same window.

League table version: Alberta, the richest province, would rank 40th among states and provinces. Ontario ranks 55th of 61, one spot above Mississippi. Quebec ranks below Mississippi.

I grew up in Edmonton and I live in San Francisco now. Same worker, two prices, and the border is the spread. C$100,000 converts to about $70,161 today. At the 2011 rate it was $101,143.
