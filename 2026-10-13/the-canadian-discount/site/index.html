The Canadian Discount

Canada's income per person, priced in American dollars. Built from IMF, World Bank (via FRED), and Statistics Canada data. See the README for the full analysis.

Three honest ways to convert it. They answer three different questions.

AT THE AIRPORT (market FX, )
What a US employer or a US bank account sees. Rate: CAD per USD.
AT THE GROCERY STORE (PPP)
What it buys if you stay in Canada, priced in US purchasing power. Uses the 2024 PPP income ratio.
THE 2011 GHOST (parity era)
This same Canadian salary converted at the 2011 average rate (0.9887 CAD per USD), when Canadian income per person peaked at of America's.

A conversion is not a verdict: taxes, health care, housing, and childcare differ by city, not just by country. The calculator prices the gap. It does not tell you to move.
