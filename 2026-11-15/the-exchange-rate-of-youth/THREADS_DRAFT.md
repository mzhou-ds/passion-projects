How many kilos is a year of youth worth?

I priced it on 877,655 powerlifting meets. At 40, one year of aging costs a 90-kilo man about 1.3 kilos of bodyweight. At 60 it costs 2.4. The price doubles between 40 and 60.

And the three lifts don't share one price. At 45, buying back a year costs 0.9 kilos on bench, 1.4 on squat, 1.8 on deadlift. Mass is twice as effective at buying bench as deadlift.

The twist: the aging curve overstates aging 4x. Lifters who keep competing past 45 lose just 0.2% a year. The 55-year-olds at a meet are mostly people who started at 50.
