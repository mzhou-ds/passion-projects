Build #52 · nutrition · Nutritional Jetlag: you differ more from yesterday's you than from a stranger
NHANES 2007-2018 (six cycles), 24,139 adults 20-79 with two reliable 24h recalls, survey-weighted (WTDR2D).

1. The shift itself: median absolute day-to-day move in the calorie-weighted midpoint of the eating
   day is 1.26 h (IQR 0.58-2.22; mean 1.60 h). 29.5% of adults move >2 h, 13.7% move >3 h.
   Eating window changes by a median 1.58 h; first bite mean |shift| 1.59 h, last bite 1.52 h.
2. Within beats between: within-person SD of the midpoint is 1.55 h vs a between-person SD of
   1.03 h. A person's mean shift (1.60 h) is 77% of the mean gap between two random strangers
   (2.07 h). 31.0% of people shift more between their two days than the entire middle-50% spread
   of people's average midpoints (IQR 1.93 h).
3. Reliability: ICC(day1, day2) = 0.31 midpoint, 0.39 first bite, 0.30 last bite, 0.32 window,
   0.26 share of kcal after 20:00, 0.45 total kcal. Even a two-day average has reliability 0.47
   for the midpoint; Spearman-Brown says ~9 recall days are needed to pin one person's midpoint
   at reliability 0.80. Single-day timing studies attenuate true associations by roughly the ICC.
4. It is not the weekend: mixed weekday/weekend pairs shift a median 1.31 h vs 1.21 h for
   both-weekday pairs (~6 min more). In mixed pairs the weekend day's midpoint is only 0.17 h
   later, its last bite is not later at all (-0.05 h), and it carries +74 kcal.
5. The day does not slide, it stretches: correlation between the signed first-bite shift and the
   signed last-bite shift is 0.06. Among people whose both ends moved, the ends move in the same
   direction 50.6% of the time, a coin flip.
6. The later day is the bigger day (within-person, weighted least squares on day differences):
   +38.4 kcal per hour the midpoint runs later (SE 4.7), +49.4 kcal per hour the window widens
   (SE 2.8), +14.3 kcal per 10-point rise in after-20:00 share. Binned: a midpoint 1-2 h later
   than your other day comes with +139 kcal relative to the average pair; >2 h earlier, -107 kcal.
   (Day 2 recalls average -102 kcal vs day 1 overall; intercepts/centering absorb that level gap.)
7. The unnamed fourth meal: snacks named as snacks carry 39.1% of all calories eaten after 20:00.
   On 16.1% of days a person eats >=300 kcal of post-20:00 "snack"; 39.6% of days have any
   post-20:00 snack, median 244 kcal when present.
8. Who wobbles: 20-somethings median 1.45 h (36.7% >2 h) vs 70-somethings 1.11 h (22.4%).
   XGBoost predicting |midpoint shift| reaches only R2 0.12 (MAE 1.03 h vs 1.04 h baseline):
   instability is mostly not demographics. Top SHAP features are the person's share of calories
   after 20:00 (0.35 h) and their average midpoint (0.23 h), then window, kcal level, age.
   Instability is flat across 2007-2018 (cycle medians 1.21-1.34 h): not a modern decay story.
