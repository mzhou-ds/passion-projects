# Diaspora data sources

All Chinese Canadian figures are compiled from Statistics Canada census data,
via two citable sources. Raw compiled series: `diaspora_history.csv`,
`diaspora_cma.csv`.

## Primary: StatCan "Portrait of the Chinese Populations in Canada" (2026)

- **Citation:** Statistics Canada, *Portrait of the Chinese Populations in
  Canada: Diversity and Socioeconomic Outcomes*, catalogue 89-657-X2026001
  (2026). Based on the 2021 Census of Population.
- **URL:** https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/statcan/89-657-x/89-657-x2026001-eng.pdf
- **Figures used:**
  - 1.7 million Chinese people in 2021 (4.7% of Canada); doubled since 1996;
    projected 2.6–3.5M by 2041.
  - Birthplace: China 47.8%, Canada 28.4%, Hong Kong 12.8%, Taiwan 4.1%,
    Southeast Asia 4.1%.
  - 97.7% live in CMAs; Toronto CMA 39.6%, Vancouver CMA 29.9%.
  - Richmond: 54.3% Chinese (highest share of any municipality in Canada);
    Markham: Chinese ~47.9%.
  - Religion: 71.7% no religion/secular, 20.2% Christian, 7.2% Buddhist.
  - 61.8% of 25–54 year-olds with bachelor's degree or higher; 84.5% live in
    owner-occupied households.

## Secondary: Wikipedia "Chinese Canadians" census tables

- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Canadians
  (tables cite individual StatCan census releases)
- **Figures used:**
  - Historical population: 1901: 17,312; 1971: 124,600; 1981: 285,800;
    1991: 633,931; 2001: 1,094,700; 2006: 1,346,510; 2011: 1,487,000;
    2016: ~1.57M; 2021: 1,713,163. ("Chinese ethnic origin, incl. partial
    ancestry" — the most consistent long-run cut.)
  - Top CMAs 2021: Toronto 631,050 (10.8%), Vancouver 474,655 (19.6%),
    Calgary 89,675 (7.0%), Montréal 89,400 (2.2%), Edmonton 60,200 (4.6%),
    Ottawa–Gatineau 43,775 (3.4%).
  - Provincial: BC 10.5% (517,805), Ontario 5.9% (821,835),
    Alberta 4.3% (177,990).
  - History: head tax 1885 ($50; $500 from 1903); Exclusion Act 1923–1947;
    166,487 Hong Kong immigrants 1988–1993; 1931 sex ratio 1,240 men per
    100 women; points system 1967.
