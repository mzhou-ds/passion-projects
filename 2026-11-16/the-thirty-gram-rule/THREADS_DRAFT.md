You've heard the rule. 30 grams of protein per meal, or it doesn't count. Spread it out, hit the number three times a day.

I tested it on 2,558 Americans with DXA-measured muscle. Adjusted lean mass is 7.83 whether you clear 30 grams at zero meals or all three. Coefficient: -0.0005. P-value: 0.99.

The 30-gram rule is a lab artifact. It measures hours in a clinic, not years in a body.

The one place timing does matter is breakfast, and it's not a threshold. Americans average 13 grams of protein at breakfast. Only 14% clear 25. The people who eat protein in the morning carry a little more muscle. The fix isn't counting meals. It's eating protein before noon.
