# Provenance

- **Shot data:** MoneyPuck public shot files, one zip per season, downloaded
  2026-10-25 from https://peter-tanner.com/moneypuck/downloads/shots_<year>.zip
  (the download host linked from https://moneypuck.com/data.htm).
  Seasons used: 2019-20 through 2024-25 (files shots_2019.zip to
  shots_2024.zip). 668,622 shot-attempt rows including playoffs;
  43,774 regular-season goals by skaters.
- **Fields used:** season, game_id, isPlayoffGame, time, period, event, goal,
  shooterPlayerId, shooterName, isHomeTeam, homeTeamCode, awayTeamCode,
  homeTeamGoals, awayTeamGoals (score before the shot), homeTeamWon, xGoal
  (MoneyPuck model), playerPositionThatDidEvent, teamCode, homeEmptyNet,
  awayEmptyNet.
- **Win probability:** NOT taken from MoneyPuck. The 2024-25 file contains a
  homeWinProbability column, but it is empty for every row in the public
  file. Win probability here is an empirical surface fit from the shot data
  itself: P(shooting team eventually wins) grouped by goal differential and
  5-minute clock bucket, regulation only, with a fixed overtime rule.
  Out-of-sample check (fit 2019-20..2022-23, scored on 2023-24..2024-25,
  238,999 shots): Brier 0.173 vs 0.250 for a constant base rate; calibration
  within 0.03 in every probability band (data/wp_calibration.csv).
- **Derived tables** in this folder are produced by src/02..05 from the raw
  zips. Raw zips and the two intermediate parquets are gitignored and
  regenerated by src/01_fetch.py and src/02_leverage.py.
