One in four NHL goals is scored after the game is already decided. Win probability past 90 percent, one way or the other.

We argue about those goals like they say something about the player. Stat padder. Empty net merchant. Or the other side, he scores when it matters.

I scored all 43,774 regular season goals from the last six seasons for leverage and ran the test trades give us for free. When a player changes teams, does his garbage time profile follow him?

It does not. A player's decided-goal share predicts his share next season at r = 0.05. Nothing. His new team's share predicts it at r = 0.34.

Johnny Gaudreau scored 42.5 percent of his goals in decided games in Calgary. First year in Columbus: 19 percent. Bo Horvat went from 36.8 in Vancouver to 18.2 with the Islanders. The profile stayed with the jersey.

Ovechkin is last among 150-goal scorers in average goal leverage. 89 of his 238 goals in this window came in decided games, 31 into empty nets. That is not a knock on him. It is a fingerprint of a good team and a coach using him to close games.

The goals are not worthless either. For teams, goal differential with garbage goals deleted predicts nothing better than the raw number. A team earns its blowouts. For players, a decided goal carries about half the signal of a coin-flip goal about next season. Discount it. Do not delete it, and do not build a personality out of it.

Garbage time is a uniform, not a trait.
