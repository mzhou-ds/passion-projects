The Release Glut

Electronic music doubled its output in fifteen years while the average release lost its audience. The median 2025 release has one Discogs collector. But the format you choose matters more than the year you release in. Try it:

A vinyl release is owned by nobody 0.6–1.4% of the time. A file release: 10–49%. An XGBoost model of collection velocity (5-fold CV R² = 0.613) ranks format first by a wide margin — ahead of age, label, and release year. The collapse is composition, not decay: the shelf changed into a format people do not collect.
