Home ice is a body clock, not a flight. I pulled 4,163 NHL games and
stripped out the schedule, and the building still wins. But the part
the schedule does explain is weird. Miles flown mean nothing. Trip
length means nothing. What matters is which way your clock moved. Fly
east three hours and the home team wins 58% of the time. Fly west three
hours and they win 49%. Same shift, opposite result. And back-to-backs
only tax the visitor. The home team sleeping in its own bed loses
nothing. Full breakdown in the repo.
