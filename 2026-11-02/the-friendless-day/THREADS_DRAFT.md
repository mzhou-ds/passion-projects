Nobody lost a little friendship. Millions of people lost the whole day.

I ran 22 years of time diaries, 250,000 days. Friend time fell from 56 minutes
a day to 26. But on days friends actually meet, the time barely moved: 242
minutes then, 227 now.

94% of the fall is days going to zero. Days with zero friend contact went
from 77% to 89%, and they never came back after 2020.

A friend day is either nothing or three hours. There is no 15-minute version.
That is why it is fragile: it needs a whole evening, and evenings are the
first thing a busy life sells.

The partner is the floor. Partner time did not fall at all. Family bent the
same way, less. Friendship is pure opt-in, so it has no floor, and it falls
to zero instead of thinning.

The fix is not a little more connection every day. It is a standing day, the
same weeknight, protected like a lease. Because the failure mode is not a
shorter evening. It is an evening that never gets scheduled.
