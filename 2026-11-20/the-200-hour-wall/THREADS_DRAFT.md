It takes 200 hours together to make a close friend. That number is famous. Nobody priced it in years.

I ran the math. A typical 35-year-old spends 114 hours a year with friends, total, split across everyone they know. Give a new person a fair one-in-five share and a close friend takes 8.8 years. On the adult-only version of the ladder it is 11.6.

Even if you gave them everything, all 114 hours, no other friends, it still takes almost two years. There is no fast path. School was the fast path and it is gone.

The fix is not meeting more people. It is concentration. Two hours a week with the same person gets you there in under two years. A monthly dinner takes eight. One long weekend together buys more hours than a year of monthly dinners.
