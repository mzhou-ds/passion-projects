The 200-Hour Wall

It takes about 200 hours together to make a close friend (Hall 2019). How many years is that for you, at the rate you actually see friends?

Annual friend hours are the ATUS 2021–25 diary average for that age band: minutes with a friend present, outside work.

Pooled ladder (students + adults): 50 / 90 / 200 hours. Adult ladder: 94 / 164 / 264 hours. Hall, J. Social and Personal Relationships 2019. The model assumes every counted hour is relaxed, one-on-one time, with no missed weeks and no decay. Real life is slower: group hangouts split the hours across several people.

What actually moves the number

Not your total social time. The share you give one person, and whether the hours are concentrated. A standing two hours a week with the same person beats five scattered hours across five people, because the clock only runs on the hours you spend with them. One long weekend together is worth more than a year of monthly dinners: about 30 waking hours versus 24.
