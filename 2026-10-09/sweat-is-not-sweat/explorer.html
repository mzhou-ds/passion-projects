Sweat is not sweat.

Your fitness app counts minutes. Your body counts context. Tell this page how you move — it answers with NHANES 2015–2018, 5,365 working-age adults, measured HbA1c and BMI, self-reported activity split by domain.

Predicted probability of rating your own health fair or poor:
–

The probability comes from a logistic model fitted on the NHANES sample (age, sex, income, leisure/work MET-minutes at GPAQ weights — vigorous 8 METs, moderate 4 — and sitting). Associations in a cross-section, not a diagnosis and not destiny: people who feel unwell also exercise less, and no slider fixes that. What the model can't manufacture is a benefit for work activity — it tried; the coefficient came back 0.001. Full method, charts, and code: see the README in this directory. Data: CDC NHANES 2015–2016 and 2017–2018 public files.
